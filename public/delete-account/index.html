Delete Your Account

Last updated: October 4, 2026

You can ask us to delete your Kurukuru account and the personal data tied to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Email CONTACT_EMAIL_HERE from the kiit.ac.in address you use for Kurukuru.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my account.
  3. We will confirm your request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile details.
  • Your ride plans, matches and trip history.
  • Your location history and map exploration progress.
  • Your coins, XP, levels and badges.

What may be kept

  • Landmark notes you planted may be kept in anonymized form so other students' maps are not broken.
  • Records we must keep to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes, for as long as the law requires.
  • Responses to the early access survey are held by Typeform. Ask us and we will request their removal.

Please note

Deletion is permanent. Your coins and progress cannot be restored afterwards.

See our Privacy Policy for more on how we handle your data.
