KURUKURU

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Kurukuru is a peer-to-peer ride-sharing app for students and the wider Kurukuru Commune. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and basic profile details you provide.
  • Ride information: start and destination points, times, repeat days, your Pilot or Co-Pilot role, matches and trip history.
  • Location data: your location while you use the app, used to match rides and to reveal areas on the campus map. If you turn on keep exploring in the background, we also collect your location while the app is closed or your screen is off, and Android shows a notification the whole time. You can turn this off at any time in the app, and we stop collecting straight away.
  • Messages: the chat messages you send to the rider you are matched with.
  • Ratings: the stars and optional note you give the other rider after a trip, and the ratings you receive.
  • Pilot verification: if you are a Pilot and choose to get verified, your vehicle type, vehicle registration number and driving licence number.
  • Payments: when coin top-ups and cash-outs are available, the amounts, order and payment references, and the UPI ID you enter to cash out. Payments are processed by Razorpay. We never receive or store your card, bank or UPI PIN details.
  • In-app activity: coins, XP, levels, badges and landmark notes you post.
  • Survey responses: details you submit through our early access form, which is hosted by Typeform.

How we use it

  • To put you in the right matching group (your college, if your confirmed email belongs to a listed one, or the Kurukuru Commune) and keep the groups separate.
  • To match Pilots and Co-Pilots and show Kuru in and Kuru out points.
  • To run coins, XP, badges and map exploration.
  • To let matched riders coordinate by chat, and to show riders each other's average rating.
  • To check Pilots' vehicle and licence details so that verified Pilots can show a verified tag.
  • To process coin top-ups and cash-outs and keep a record of them.
  • To maintain safety, prevent misuse and improve the app.
  • To contact you about early access and service updates.

What other users can see

A Co-Pilot sees only their own Kuru in and Kuru out points. A Pilot's full route is not shared with Co-Pilots. Landmark notes you plant are visible to other students, without your name. The rider you are matched with can read your chat messages and sees your average rating and, for Pilots, a verified tag. Your vehicle and licence numbers, ratings notes and payment details are never shown to other riders.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run Kurukuru (such as hosting, database, payment (Razorpay) and form providers), when required by law, or to protect users and the service.

Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service, then delete or anonymize it unless the law requires otherwise.

Your choices

You can delete your account and data yourself in the app (vault box, then Delete my account) or by contacting us below. Deleting removes your profile, flags, ride plans, location and map progress, and any vehicle and licence details. Chat messages, ratings and payment records tied to a trip you shared with another student, or that we must keep by law, may be kept without your name. You can also turn off background location in the app, and ask to correct your information.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no system is completely secure.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
